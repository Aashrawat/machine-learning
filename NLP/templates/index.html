The page cleans the words the same way as the notebook, then asks three models to pick an emotion: sadness, anger, love, surprise, fear, or joy.

Word counts + Naive Bayes

Each word becomes a count. Naive Bayes picks the emotion those counts fit best.

TF-IDF + Naive Bayes

The same Naive Bayes model, with words that show up in many sentences weighed down.

TF-IDF + logistic regression

A learned weight for every word. The emotion with the largest score wins. This one is the most accurate on the held-out sentences.

Models are fit on an 80/20 split of train.txt (random_state=42). A sentence you type is scored with those fitted models.
